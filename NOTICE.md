## What this distribution changes

- Rebranded as *Seeker Gravity* (`com.justinliehu.seekergravity`): new title wordmark, boot splash,
  icons, and store artwork. None of the original ROTA logo, the Harmony Monroe logo, or the
  author's website mark are shipped.
- Steam integration removed (`addons/steam_api`, achievements, store overlay); itch.io / Flatpak
  packaging scripts removed.
- Android build switched to the prebuilt Godot 3.6.1 export template, arm64 only.
- Gameplay, levels, music and sound are unchanged.
